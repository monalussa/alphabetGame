# Credits

## Voices (speech generated with Piper, https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl)
- French: `fr_FR-siwis-medium`, trained on the SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database
  (University of Edinburgh), licensed CC BY 4.0 — https://datashare.is.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
- English: `en_US-ljspeech-high`, trained on the LJ Speech Dataset (public domain) — https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
- Croatian: recorded by the project owner.

Piper itself (GPL-3.0) is only used as a build tool; it is not shipped with the app.

## Sounds
Fun sounds (boing, pop, fart, whistle) are synthesized by `tools/` — our own work.

## Pictures
Placeholder emoji are rendered by the visitor's system font. Reviewed pictures and their licenses are
listed in `content/images/manifest.json` (Phase 3).
