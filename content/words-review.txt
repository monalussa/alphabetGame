ALPHABETGAME – WORD LIST FOR REVIEW
=================================
Format:  LETTER : suggested 3 words   | alternatives
Mark what you like, cross out what you don't, add your own.
[!] = phonics note: the word starts with that letter, but the first SOUND differs
      (may confuse a child who is learning sounds).
SKIP = not enough good, picturable child words → fun sound in the app instead.

------------------------------------------------------------
FRANÇAIS (FR)
------------------------------------------------------------
A : ananas, avion, abeille          | arbre, âne, araignée
B : ballon, banane, bateau          | bébé, biberon, bus
C : canard, camion, carotte         | cochon, clown, crocodile
    [!] avoid "chat"/"cheval" for C: "ch" is a different sound
D : dauphin, dinosaure, dragon      | dent, domino
E : éléphant, étoile, escargot      | échelle, épée
    [!] most E words start with "é"; this is fine if E is presented as "é"
F : fraise, fleur, fusée            | fourmi, fromage, feu
G : gâteau, guitare, gorille        | grenouille, gant
    [!] avoid "girafe" (soft g /ʒ/)
H : hibou, hérisson, hélicoptère    | hamster
    [!] H is silent in French, so all H words sound like they start with a vowel
I : igloo, île, iguane              | image
J : jupe, jus, jaguar               | jardin, jongleur, journal
K : koala, kangourou, kiwi          | kayak, karaté
L : lion, lapin, lune               | lait, livre, loup
M : maison, mouton, moto            | main, mouche, melon
N : nuage, nez, nid                 | nounours, noix
O : orange, ours, olive             | oreille, os
    [!] avoid "oiseau" (oi = /wa/), "œuf"
P : pomme, poisson, papillon        | poule, pingouin, pizza
Q : quille, queue, quatre           | quiche
    (Q is weak but usable; "queue" = a cat's tail)
R : robot, renard, raisin           | radis, requin, rat
S : soleil, serpent, souris         | sapin, salade
T : tortue, train, tomate           | tigre, téléphone
U : ukulélé, usine, uniforme        | (weak letter: consider SKIP)
V : vache, vélo, voiture            | valise, volcan
W : SKIP (only wagon, wapiti)
X : SKIP (only xylophone)
Y : yaourt, yoyo, yéti              | yacht, yak
Z : zèbre, zoo, zéro                | zigzag
é è à ç ù keys: fun sound (fart / boing / pop)
